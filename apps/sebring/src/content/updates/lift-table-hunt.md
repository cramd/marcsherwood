At 123 kg dry, the Sebring needs a proper lift table — ideally 1,000 lb capacity. We are watching Princess Auto for Powerfist and Pro.Point deals, and keeping an eye on used Titan lifts on the Lower Mainland.

Until the lift arrives, the center stand and a good set of blocks will have to do.
