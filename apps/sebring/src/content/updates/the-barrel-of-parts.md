The restoration starts with what we have — and what we do not. Dad rode a Sebring 350 in his early riding days; this is the same model, decades later, in our hands.

We are cataloging everything from the parts barrel using factory assembly codes. Each bin gets a label, each part gets a photo ID, and we track status from *wanted* through *ready* for assembly.

Next up: ultrasonic cleaning on the valve covers and a deep dive into the Dell'Orto carb body.
